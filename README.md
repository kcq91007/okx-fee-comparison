## What makes a crypto app one of the best?

The “best” crypto app is rarely the one with the longest list of coins. A useful app should match your intended workflow.

Before opening an account, compare these areas:

- **Availability:** Can residents in your country or state use the relevant services?
- **Fiat funding:** Can you deposit and withdraw through a bank account or payment method you already use?
- **Trading fees:** What do maker and taker fees cost at your expected trading volume?
- **Coin selection:** Does the platform list the assets you want?
- **Security controls:** Are two-factor authentication, withdrawal protections, device controls, and identity verification available?
- **Order types:** Can you use limit orders, stop orders, recurring buys, or advanced charting?
- **Wallet access:** Does the app include a self-custody wallet or Web3 functionality?
- **Transfer costs:** Are network and withdrawal fees clearly shown before you confirm?
- **Tax and account records:** Can you download transaction history and account statements?

A crypto app can be excellent in one category and frustrating in another. Robinhood, for example, may appeal to people who already use it for stocks and want a familiar interface. Coinbase is often easier for a first-time buyer. Kraken and OKX are more relevant to users who want trading tools beyond a basic buy button.

That is why a single universal ranking is usually misleading. The right choice depends on whether your priority is simplicity, fees, trading depth, automation, or access to Web3 products.

## Security steps to take before trading

Whichever app you choose, set up account security before depositing money.

Use a unique password and enable two-factor authentication. Prefer an authenticator app or hardware security key where supported instead of relying only on SMS.

Also consider:

- Adding an anti-phishing code if the platform supports one
- Reviewing logged-in devices regularly
- Enabling withdrawal address allowlisting where available
- Keeping most long-term holdings away from an actively used trading account
- Testing a small withdrawal before transferring a larger amount
- Checking the network before sending assets
- Saving transaction records for tax reporting
- Avoiding links sent through unsolicited direct messages

Referral links should lead to the intended signup flow. If a page redirects to an unexpected domain or asks for a wallet recovery phrase, stop. An exchange registration page should not need your existing self-custody wallet seed phrase.
